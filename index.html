            Welcome to Pet Streak!

            Raise a cute red panda together with your friends. Here's how it works.

            🍎

            Feed it every day

            Tap Feed once a day. When 2 of you feed on the same day, your pet earns XP, its 🔥 streak grows, and you each get 🐾 paws.

            🤝

            Raise it together

            XP only counts when 2 people feed, so invite a friend as a co-parent straight away. Up to 4 of you can share a pet.

            🥺

            Don't miss a day

            A missed day makes your pet hungry. More missed days make it sick and it loses levels. After a week it runs away to the sanctuary. A ❄️ freeze saves your streak for one missed day.

            ✨

            Have fun with it

            Feed its favourite food 💭, play with it, bathe it when it's muddy 🛁 and dress it up 🎀. Bonus XP helps it rank up for big paw rewards.

            📲

            Get the Pet Streak app

            Put Pet Streak on your home screen: it opens like a normal app, full screen, and can remind you to feed your pet.

              How it was made

              1. Base model. Modelled, retopologised and textured in Blender.
              2. Set up to move. Pivot set at the feet, with the eyes, eyelids and lashes kept as separate parts so they can move on their own.
              3. Eyes. Eyeballs that each turn on their own centre, with a big glossy pupil and a warm copper iris.
              4. Eyelids and lashes. Plain upper lids shaped to fit each eye opening, with black lashes that ride on the lid edge.
              5. Optimised for the web. Metallic and normal maps dropped, the colour map cut from 4K to 2K and roughness to 1K, and both eyes share one small texture.
              6. One file. Exported as a single 1.13 MB GLB and shown here with three.js.

              What it does

              • Follows your cursor, eyes first, with the head turning a little after.
              • Blinks on its own every few seconds. Tap it to make it blink.
              • Lowers its lids when it looks down, and breathes gently when idle.
              • Drag to turn it, scroll or pinch to zoom, or jump to an eyes close-up.
              • On a phone, drag to make it look around, switch to Rotate to turn it, or turn on Tilt and tilt your phone.
              • Switch between Baked (lighting baked into the texture) and Lit (live lights), and try three backdrops.
